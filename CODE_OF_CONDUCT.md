# Code Of Conduct

## Our Commitment

ForgeFlow contributors, maintainers, and community participants commit to making
participation respectful, professional, and free from harassment for everyone,
regardless of experience, identity, background, disability, appearance, nationality,
religion, or other personal characteristics.

## Expected Behavior

- Discuss ideas and code with empathy and technical clarity.
- Critique contributions rather than contributors.
- Respect different experience levels and communication styles.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Protect private security reports and personal information.
- Accept maintainers' reasonable decisions about scope and community safety.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized attention.
- Personal attacks, insults, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately unsafe instructions, malicious code, or deceptive test evidence.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Enforcement

Report community-conduct concerns privately to
[idairen@qq.com](mailto:idairen@qq.com). Do not use this address as a substitute
for the vulnerability process in `SECURITY.md`.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, reject
contributions, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation.
Reports will be handled as confidentially as practicable. Enforcement decisions are
based on impact, context, repetition, and community safety.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, pull requests, issues, releases,
community spaces, and public representation of the ForgeFlow project.
